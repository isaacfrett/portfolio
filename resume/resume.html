Quantitative Futures Trading Platform

Archived

Sole architect and engineer · overview: github.com/isaacfrett/quant-public

End-to-end research and execution platform that discovered, validated and deployed systematic futures strategies under anti-overfitting controls, then ran them against a broker API with hard risk governance. Built so that most candidates fail.

  • Modeling. LightGBM over a 700+ feature panel from 50+ modular builders, on liquid CME macro futures, intraday on the 15-minute bar with 1h/4h/daily context. Two-stage side/meta architecture — a side model predicts direction, a meta model decides whether to act — with probabilities isotonically calibrated before position sizing.
  • Validation. Walk-forward CV with purge and embargo, plus combinatorial purged CV for distribution-level out-of-sample estimates. Triple-barrier and volatility-normalised labels, de-overlapped so no training label reads prices inside a test window.
  • Gating. L1 signal gates then L2 economic gates — net-of-cost Sharpe, deflated Sharpe, and probability of backtest overfitting. Commissions and slippage netted before any statistic is computed, so the gates judged tradable edge rather than paper edge.
  • Integrity as a release gate. An automated lookahead auditor cross-checked every feature's first-valid index against the label horizon, and continuous backtest-to-live parity tests meant divergence stopped a change from shipping. Leakage was a release-blocking defect.
  • Execution and operations. Bracketed order lifecycle with a risk governor — per-trade caps, exposure limits, drawdown halts, file-based kill switch — operated by a fleet of specialised agents across role-separated servers, surfaced through a real-time React/TypeScript dashboard over FastAPI.
